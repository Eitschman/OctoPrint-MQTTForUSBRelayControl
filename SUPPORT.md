# Support

No public release, support address or repository has been approved yet. Once a
public repository exists, use its issue templates for sanitized diagnostics and
reproduction steps. Do not post credentials, configuration dumps or private URLs.
Consult [troubleshooting](docs/public/troubleshooting.md) first. Compatibility reports
should distinguish tested behavior from expected behavior and include exact versions.
Contributions of reviewed fixes, portable tests, translations and authorized hardware
reports can support maintenance. No donation service is configured.
